How it works

One click from the title screen to the arena

There is no server to join and no world to download. The mod carries the practice world and the bots inside it.

1

Add the mod

The app asks which launcher you use and walks you through it. On Modrinth and the standard launcher it installs the file for you.

2

Launch, then press Connect

Start Minecraft 1.21.11 with Fabric. The app finds the running game on your computer, connects to it and opens the practice world for you.

3

Pick a drill or a duel

Choose it in the app and it starts in your game. Your score, medals and mastery update live while you play.

Gamemodes and skill branches

Start with two kits. Earn the rest.

Every mode has three scored drills and a five-tier bot ladder from Rookie to Ace. Reach 80% mastery and beat the Elite bot to unlock a mode's branches.

Gamemode

Mace

The base MacePvP kit: Density and Breach maces, wind charges, sword and axe, shield, pearls, and an elytra with no rockets.

  • Launch & Smash: total smash damage over 10 attempts
  • Moving Target: smashes on a dodging dummy in 60 seconds
  • Smash Chain: longest back-to-back chain
Gamemode

Crystal

Standard Crystal PvP kit on real, breakable ground that craters as you fight: netherite, totems, end crystals, obsidian, anchors and glowstone. The bot places obsidian, crystals and anchors, pearls after you, and punishes a popped totem.

  • Place & Pop: crystals detonated in 30 seconds
  • Totem Breaker: pops on a dummy that re-totems instantly
  • Under Fire: survive a bot that crystals you non-stop
Mace branch

Spear

Mace kit without the elytra, plus a Lunge III spear. Built around attribute swapping: select the spear and jab in the same tick so the lunge cooldown almost disappears.

  • Lunge Lines: lunge through rings
  • Attribute Swap: lunges in 30 seconds
  • Lunge Into Smash: close the gap, launch, smash
Unlocks when you master Mace
Mace branch

Elytra + Mace

Mace kit plus rockets. Wings on, climb, wait a beat, look back down, rocket at the target, wings off at the last moment, smash.

  • Takeoff: fastest climb to the marker
  • Dive Bomb: smash damage over 6 dives
  • Full Sequence: clean dives on a moving target
Unlocks when you master Mace
Bots that scale

Rookie to Ace

One difficulty number drives reaction time, aim error, crit timing, how often the bot dives, how fast it re-totems and how quickly it places and pops crystals. The Ace spear bot re-lunges as fast as a player who has the swap down.

Install the mod

Pick your launcher

The app shows this same guide on first launch, with the mod file ready to drag out. You always need a Fabric 1.21.11 profile.

  1. Open the Profiles tab and create a new profile: Java, then Custom profile.
  2. Keep the loader on Fabric, pick game version 1.21.11 and create it.
  3. Press the gear on the profile, then Edit. Open the profile folder and drop the PVPTraining jar into mods.
  4. Launch the profile, wait for the title screen, then press Connect in the app.

Dawn is the MCPVP launcher that replaced Feather. It is new, so button names may shift between updates.

  1. Open the version selector, choose 1.21.11 and select the Fabric add-on.
  2. Press the gear at the bottom right, then Mods.
  3. Drag the PVPTraining jar onto the Mods panel and keep it enabled.
  4. Press Launch, wait for the title screen, then press Connect in the app.

Lunar only loads your own mods when the Fabric add-on is selected.

  1. Create an instance with loader Fabric and game version 1.21.11.
  2. In the PVPTraining app, pick that instance and press Install. By hand: instance, Content, the arrow beside Install content, Add from file.
  3. Press Play, wait for the title screen, then press Connect in the app.

The instance has to be a Fabric one. A vanilla instance does not show mod options.

  1. Install Fabric for 1.21.11. This video walks through it: How To Download & Install Fabric Mods (1.21.11).
  2. Put the PVPTraining jar in your mods folder. With the standard Minecraft Launcher the app can do this for you.
  3. Start the Fabric 1.21.11 installation, wait for the title screen, then press Connect in the app.

Default folders: %APPDATA%\.minecraft\mods on Windows, ~/Library/Application Support/minecraft/mods on macOS.

FAQ

Good to know

Why only 1.21.11?

The spear, Lunge and the current attribute-swap timing all live in 1.21.11, and the bots are written against that exact version. The mod refuses to load on anything else so you never train against wrong mechanics. More versions will follow, and the greyed-out version picker in the app becomes a real one when they do.

Will this get me banned anywhere?

The mod does nothing on servers. It only acts inside its own single-player practice world. If a server or launcher forbids unknown mods, use a separate profile for training.

How does the app find my game?

When Minecraft starts with the mod, it opens a connection that only this computer can reach and writes a small file with a one-time key into your user folder. The app reads that key to connect. Nothing leaves your machine.

Can I use my own hotbar layout?

Yes. In the practice world run /kit edit mace (or cpvp, spear, elytra) to get the standard kit, arrange it how you like, then /kit save main mace. Your whole inventory is saved and loaded every time a drill or duel in that mode starts. /kit list, /kit use, /kit default and /kit delete manage them. Bots always use the standard kit.

How do updates work?

Every time the app starts it checks GitHub for the latest release. A newer mod is pulled straight away and dropped into any mods folder the app installed it to. A newer app downloads in the background: press Restart to update, or just close the app and it installs itself. Every download is checked against the checksum GitHub publishes for it.

How do branches unlock?

Mastery is half drill medals and half the toughest bot you have beaten in a first-to-3 duel. Reach 80% in Mace and beat the Elite bot, and Spear and Elytra + Mace unlock. Crystal has no branches yet.

Can I train without the app open?

Yes. Once you are in the PVPTraining world, /pvpt start mace drill mace_smash or /pvpt start crystal duel veteran starts a session, and /pvpt stop ends it.
